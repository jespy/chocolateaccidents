HOW TO CONTAIN PRODUCTION

1. INSTALL EQUIPMENT

Choose factory equipment, then move the ghost machine over open factory floor and click to install. Green range means valid; red means blocked. There is no placement grid.

2. STOP DEFECTS

Defects follow the yellow production route toward the Main Tempering Tank. Destroy them before Batch Integrity reaches 0%.

3. IMPROVISE

Upgrade machines, remove bad placements, and use Emergency Shutdown or Espresso Break when production gets unreasonable.

4. SURVIVE 10 INCIDENTS

The final incident contains a major manufacturing disaster. Management would prefer the tank remain usable.

Controls: Mouse to build/select • Esc cancels placement/selection, then pauses • 1 Emergency Shutdown • 2 Espresso Break
